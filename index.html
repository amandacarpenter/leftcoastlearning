Flagship Product

Remedy508

"Not Accessible, Not Acceptable™"

An AI-powered accessibility platform that automatically remediates documents, PDFs, videos, and images into WCAG 2.1 AA-compliant materials. Purpose-built for higher education institutions.

Go to Remedy508 View pricing
  • Document Fixer Instantly remediates Word docs and PDFs to meet WCAG 2.1 AA — headings, alt text, reading order, and more.
  • Complex PDF Handles multi-column layouts, tables, and form-heavy PDFs that standard tools can't touch.
  • Video Accessibility Generates accurate captions and transcripts so your video content is compliant and searchable.
  • Canvas Review Audits your Canvas course for accessibility issues and delivers a clear, prioritized fix list.
  • AI Alt Text Uses AI to write WCAG-compliant image descriptions automatically — no manual writing required.
